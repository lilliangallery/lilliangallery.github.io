A life between cultures

The artist

Between two worlds,
at home in art.

I am Lillian Zhang, a Chinese painter currently based in Malta. My practice is rooted in traditional Chinese painting, including Gongbi, Baimiao, flower-and-bird painting, and Dunhuang-inspired work.

Living in Malta has brought new colours, textures, architecture, and ways of seeing into my life. I am interested in the quiet connections between cultures, and in art as a space for attention, reflection, and understanding.
